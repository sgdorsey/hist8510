Liberty
File

September 9th
10:30 AM -
1941 F.D.

Dear Eleanor Roosevelt,
Recently, I scanned
your article, concerning
Conscription for girls.
Defintely, your idea is
superb, for if our
government is not interest-
ed in our womanhood,
then who is?? I do not
believe any woman would
intentionally sell her soul
for a mess of pottage. I
am so in hopes our Girl
Youth shall be advised
